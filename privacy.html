Privacy Policy – Custo 3D

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Data we collect: none. The app requires no account and does not collect personal data, use ads or analytics.

Data stored on your device: everything you enter (prices, printer settings, quotes, orders, customers and finance entries) is saved only on your phone. It is not sent to us or to third parties, and it is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data. Backups you export are shared only where you choose.

Internet access: the app connects to the internet only to (1) look up electricity rates from Brazil's ANEEL open-data portal or a public copy of that data on GitHub, for users in Brazil, sending only the selected utility name, and (2) download a public list of filament store links from GitHub. No personal data is sent.

Subscription: the Custo 3D Pro subscription is billed and managed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether your subscription is active. You can cancel anytime in Play Store → Payments & subscriptions.

Store links: the app may show affiliate links to filament stores. We may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Once you open a store, its own privacy policy applies.

Children: the app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Contact: questions can be sent to the contact e-mail shown on the app's Google Play page.
